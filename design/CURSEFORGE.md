# Tallymaster

**Tallymaster** is a lightweight, always-on tracker for the stuff you actually care about hoarding — **items, currencies, and collectibles** — shown on screen, grouped by category, and tallied across your **entire account**.

Crafting reagents, weekly currencies, that one rare drop you're farming, mounts and pets you're chasing — pin them to a tidy on-screen list and always know how many you have, on every character, at a glance.

---

## Features

- **Always-on tracker** — a compact, movable on-screen list of everything you're tracking. It remembers where you put it and only grows downward as you expand groups.
- **Track almost anything** — items, currencies, mounts, battle pets, and transmog appearances, each counted with the right source (bags, bank, equipped, mail, collection state, and more).
- **Account-wide counts** — see the combined total across all your characters, or switch to per-character. Hover any entry for a per-character breakdown grouped by realm.
- **Add it in seconds** — type a name or ID, or just **Shift-click an item or currency** straight into the add box. A live details panel shows the icon, quality, crafting tier, item level, type, stack size, sell price, expansion, and bind type before you commit.
- **Crafting-quality aware** — for items with a crafting quality (Tier 1–3), choose whether an entry counts **only that tier** or **every quality** of the item.
- **Category grouping** — entries are auto-grouped by category with collapsible headers, or flatten it into a single list. Sort alphabetically or by count.
- **Known items browser** — a searchable, category-filterable window of everything you've ever added, with sortable Name / Count columns.
- **ElvUI support** — skins cleanly when ElvUI is installed; no-op otherwise.

## Getting started

1. Open the **Add** window — click the minimap button, press your keybind, or type `/tally`.
2. Type an item/currency **name or ID**, or **Shift-click** one from your bags. Press **Add**.
3. It appears on the tracker. Drag the tracker wherever you like.

**Tip:** Set your count scope to *Account-wide* in the settings to see totals across all your characters. Each character contributes its counts the first time you log into it.

---

## Access it your way

**Minimap button**
- **Click** — open the Add window
- **Shift-Click** — show/hide the tracker
- **Ctrl-Click** — open Known items
- **Right-Click** — options

**Keybindings** (Key Bindings → Tallymaster): Open Add window, Toggle tracker, Toggle known items.

**Slash commands**
- `/tally` — open the Add window
- `/tally tracker` — show/hide the tracker
- `/tally known` — open the Known items browser
- `/tally config` — open the options

---

## Options

Count scope (per-character / account-wide), sort order, group-by-category, on-screen tooltip and per-character count breakdown, minimap button, and ElvUI skinning — all in the addon's settings panel.
